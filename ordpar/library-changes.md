# Changes to the Ordpar dictionary

What has changed in the dictionary's content, newest first: newly translated articles, our corrections of Bokmålsordboka's text, and each newer copy of Bokmålsordboka's data, the work of Språkrådet and the University of Bergen. With a newer copy, the articles not yet translated follow it at once, with our corrections kept wherever the text they correct is unchanged; the translated ones keep the Norwegian their English was written for until we revise them. Today's totals are on the [About page](https://ordpar.no/about/#how-far-it-has-come).

## 2026-11-10

- **Bokmålsordboka's data of 10 November 2026** (Språkrådet and the University of Bergen).
- **Our corrections of Bokmålsordboka's text now reach the articles not yet translated too,** and there are 577 more of them:
  typos, Nynorsk forms, missing words and references to the wrong sense or article, each noted at
  the article's foot.
- **Totals:** our corrections of typos, punctuation and formatting 624 in 611 articles (was 108 in 105);
  cross-references 86 in 84 articles (was 84 in 82); other corrections 64 in 64 articles (was 5 in 5).

## 2026-11-09

- **337 more articles translated into English.**
- **Totals:** 22,211 translated (+337), 36.7 % of the running words (was 36.3 %); our corrections of typos,
  punctuation and formatting 108 in 105 articles (was 107 in 104).

## 2026-10-31

- **Bokmålsordboka's "Se:" lines restored on fixed expressions.** The line that names the words a fixed expression is
  built from (*aksle seg*: "Se: aksle") had been left out of our copy; it is now kept, in Bokmålsordboka's own words,
  and shown on 8,909 fixed expressions' own pages, translated or not.
- Totals unchanged.

## 2026-10-30, second update

- **Nine typos corrected in the lines over compound lists,** in *høst*, *klinikk*, *pølse*, *reim*, *sko*, *skriv*,
  *slave*, *tun* and *verk*: Bokmålsordboka's "som etterldd i ord som", "som etterledd ord som" and the like now read
  *som etterledd i ord som*, each with our note on the correction.
- **Totals:** our corrections of typos, punctuation and formatting 107 in 104 articles (was 98 in 96); the rest
  unchanged.

## 2026-10-30

- ***ha*'s English equivalents:** "to keep" removed from the short summary at the top, which now reads "to have".
  The article translates *ha* as "keep" only once, so it is not one of the word's main equivalents.
- Totals unchanged.

## 2026-10-29

- **Bokmålsordboka's data of 29 October 2026** (Språkrådet and the University of Bergen).
- **Totals:** our corrections of typos, punctuation and formatting 98 in 96 articles (was 97 in 95); the rest
  unchanged.

## 2026-10-23 — the starting point

The totals when this list began, with Bokmålsordboka's data of 25 September 2026. No changes are listed: later entries
count from here.

- **Articles:** 94,197, of which 21,873 have been translated into English (23.2 %): 10,132 complete, with the short
  summary at the top, and 11,741 with every definition and example in English, their summary still to come.
- **Share of the text translated:** 36.3 % of the dictionary's running words (495,400 of 1,365,856), across its
  definitions, examples and etymologies.
- **Our corrections of Bokmålsordboka's text:** 190 in 185 articles (82 cross-references in 80 articles; 99 typos,
  punctuation and formatting errors in 97; the more common spelling of the headword put first in 4; 5 other
  corrections in 5). Each corrected article says so at its foot.
- **Example sentences of our own,** added where a sense had no example: 450 in 271 articles, each marked with a small
  raised "+".
