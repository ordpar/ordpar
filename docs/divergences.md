# Where Ordpar differs from ordbokene.no

Ordpar (<https://ordpar.no>) is a Norwegian–English dictionary built on *Bokmålsordboka*, the
Bokmål dictionary that the University of Bergen and the Language Council of Norway publish at
[ordbokene.no](https://ordbokene.no) under CC BY 4.0. Each Ordpar article is a Bokmålsordboka
article with English added under it; an article we have not yet translated is shown whole in
Norwegian. The site has been public since 25 October 2026 and is still being built.

**Our baseline is ordbokene.no.** For presentation and lexicography we follow what ordbokene.no
shows (its features, layout, orderings and table shapes) and check our pages against it. Where
Ordpar adds or changes something, it does so on purpose, for a reason we have written down. This
document lists those differences as of 12 November 2026, and after the tables, the features
ordbokene.no has that Ordpar lacks, with our reason where there is one. (Ordpar's Anki flashcard
deck is not covered.)

Ordpar mirrors what ordbokene.no shows, never how it is built: none of ordbokene's code is in
Ordpar. What we say about ordbokene.no is what we saw on its pages and in its API's answers, on
the date given.

Everything in the tables is live on ordpar.no unless the row says otherwise.

A few words used throughout:

- **Translated** and ***Norwegian only***: an article is either translated (its English is
  complete) or shown whole in Norwegian, marked *Norwegian only*; no article is shown
  half-translated. (*Norwegian only* is also a setting that hides all the English; it has a row
  of its own.)
- **Summary gloss**: the short list of English equivalents we write for a translated article,
  shown under its headword (*bli*: "to become · to stay, to remain · to be (forming the passive)").
- **Set phrase**: a fixed expression (*fy faen*, *holde seg à jour*), ordbokene's "faste
  uttrykk". Ordbokene treats each as an article of its own and also prints it inside the
  articles of its words.
- **Suggestions**: the list under the search box while typing. **Similar words**: the list of
  near spellings shown after a search (ordbokene's "Lignende oppslagsord").
- **Small article**: a short version of an article, opened on the page by clicking a
  cross-reference.
- **How common a word is**: from the National Library of Norway's 2026 n-gram counts over books
  since 1990 (public domain, CC0), grouped in bands.

## What ordbokene.no has that Ordpar lacks

Features of ordbokene.no that Ordpar does not have, with our reason where there is one. Each is
as ordbokene.no showed it on the date given.

- **Advanced search.** A page of its own, "Avansert søk", that searches the headwords, their
  inflected forms or the full text of the articles ("Fritekstsøk"), filters by word class, takes
  wildcards (`*` and `%` for any run of letters, `_` for one) and `|` between alternative words,
  and shows its results a page at a time: `hus*` in Bokmål finds 148 articles; a setting chooses
  which of the three searches it starts with (checked 11 and 12 November 2026). Ordpar: planned,
  but not a priority.
- **Suggestions that contain the word.** After the words that begin with what is typed, its
  suggestions list headwords and set phrases that contain it further on: *krone* gives *-krone*,
  *-kroner* and *kaste mynt og krone*; *gikk* gives *så gikk vi da* (checked 12 November 2026).
  Ordpar suggests only words that begin with what is typed; the other kind was considered in
  October 2026 and left for later.
- **Inflected forms in the suggestions.** Typing an inflected form suggests its word: *gikk*
  gives *gå* (checked 12 November 2026). Ordpar's suggestions are headwords only, though a search
  for *gikk* leads to *gå* (see "Inflected forms, with their articles"). Ordpar: perhaps later,
  not a priority.
- **A link back to the search results.** An article page opened from a search has "Tilbake til
  søkeresultatene" above the article (checked 12 November 2026). Ordpar's has "← Home" (see "A
  link to the word's other articles"). No decision yet.
- **The printed dictionaries' inflection codes.** A setting, "Vis bøyningskodene fra de trykte
  ordbøkene", off by default, adds each word's code from the printed editions after its word
  class in search results ("substantiv intetkjønn (n1)", "verb (v1)"; checked 11 and 12 November
  2026). Ordpar: left out in September 2026, as part of the printed editions' apparatus; it may
  be added later, at low priority.
- **The interface in other languages.** Its interface is in Bokmål, Nynorsk, English or
  Ukrainian (checked 12 November 2026). Ordpar's is in English only; a Norwegian interface is
  planned for its version 1.0.
- **A search shortcut.** Shift+7, which types "/" on a Norwegian keyboard, puts the cursor in the
  search box from anywhere on a page; a setting turns it off (checked 12 November 2026). No
  decision yet.
- **The search word selected after a search.** After a search the word stays in the box,
  selected, so the next word typed replaces it; a setting turns this off (checked 12 November
  2026). Ordpar keeps the cursor in the box on a computer but does not select the word. No
  decision yet.
- **A featured article and the newest words on the front page.** A featured article from each
  dictionary ("Smakebit fra Bokmålsordboka") and the five newest headwords of each ("Nytt i
  Bokmålsordboka") (checked 11 and 12 November 2026). Ordpar's front page has neither. No
  decision yet.
- **A button back to the top.** After scrolling down a long article, a round button, "Til
  toppen", in the lower corner (checked 12 November 2026). No decision yet.
- **Links to other sites marked.** A small icon after each link that leaves the site (checked 12
  November 2026). No decision yet.
- **An accessibility page and statement.** A link "Tilgjengelighet" to a help page on
  accessibility at the top of every page, beside the link that skips to the content, and an
  accessibility statement ("Tilgjengelighetserklæring", on uustatus.no) in the footer (checked 12
  November 2026). Ordpar has the skip link, but neither page. No decision yet.

## Ours by design: the identity markers

Following ordbokene's layout closely is fine, but a few things stay Ordpar's own, so that no
reader can mistake one site for the other. These need no further reason:

- **The colour.** Our default scheme is Wine (`#6b0f2e`), with a coloured night mode, and a
  second scheme of our own, Burnt orange, is offered; ordbokene's maroon is offered only as an
  option named for its source, with its own black-and-white night mode.
- **The name and the icon** (Ordpar, the "Ord / Par" patches).
- **The footer**: the credit to Bokmålsordboka and the statement that Ordpar is independent of
  the University of Bergen and the Language Council of Norway, without their logos.
- **The headword typeface**: sans-serif semibold, where ordbokene's is serif. An optional
  typography closer to ordbokene's is under consideration.
- **The English content**, which is Ordpar's own.
